How it works
Step 01

Describe your startup

Tell Goldo your sector, funding stage, where you operate, and your target raise — in a sentence or through quick prompts.

Step 02

The rules screen every investor

Hard filters drop investors who can't fit your stage or geography. A weighted scorer ranks whoever survives on sector, stage, cheque size and focus.

Step 03

Get a shortlist with reasons

Goldo returns the best-fit investors and tells you exactly why each one matched — plus a contact route where one is on file.

The method

Not a guess from a language model. A transparent expert system.

Goldo represents each investor as a structured frame and matches with two tiers of production rules. The model only reads your description — the ranking is done by rules you can inspect, so every recommendation is explainable.

Eliminators · hard filters

Stage & geography

An investor is dropped only on positive evidence of a mismatch — a growth-only fund for a pre-seed startup, or a market they don't cover. Missing data never eliminates.

Scorers · weighted

Sector, stage, cheque, focus

Survivors earn points for exact and adjacent fits. A sector-agnostic or unpriced investor is never penalised for what they simply didn't disclose.
