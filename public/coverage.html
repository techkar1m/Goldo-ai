Known gaps

What this data can't tell you

Publishing the weaknesses is part of the method. These are the ones that would change how you read a match:

Contacts

37 contact routes need verifying

Every investor has a contact route on file, but 37 carry a verification flag — usually a generic inbox or an address inferred from a firm's domain rather than confirmed. Goldo shows you the flag; check before you send anything.

Cheque sizes

Only 34 publish a range

142 investors have no usable ticket range, and some of the 34 are estimated from published fund size rather than stated per-deal cheques. That is why rule S4 is worth only 10 points and never eliminates.

Vocabulary

One stray sector label

A single investor is tagged with the label tech, which sits outside the fifteen sectors the classifier can produce. It can never win a sector match — it still competes on stage, geography and cheque size.

Freshness

A snapshot, not a feed

The base was compiled by hand and is not continuously refreshed. Funds change thesis, raise new vehicles, and go quiet. Treat a match as a starting point for your own research.
