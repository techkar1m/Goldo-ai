Matching vocabulary

The rules can only match what both sides name

A classifier label that never appears in the data can't match anything, and a value in the data outside the label list can never be matched to. These three vocabularies are the contract between your description and the knowledge base.

Data quality

Frames are unevenly filled, and the rule weights are set to reflect that. This is the ordering that matters when you read a match:

Roughly a quarter declare a stage. It stays the heaviest scorer at 35 points because when it is present it is the most reliable signal there is. The other three quarters survive rule E1 untouched — missing data never eliminates.

Worth a full 25 points when a genuine overlap exists, and deliberately neutral for the 4,699 that declare nothing.

331 of 6,319

The thinnest slot by far, and the one most likely to disappoint. A strong match with no contact route is still a real research lead — you just have to find the door yourself.

0 of 6,319

Cheque size · none

No investor in the current base publishes a per-deal range, so rule S4 never awards its 10 points. Fund sizes exist for a handful of firms but are not the same thing as a cheque, and Goldo will not estimate one from the other. The rule stays in the engine for when better data arrives.

Known gaps

What this data can't tell you

Publishing the weaknesses is part of the method. These are the ones that should change how you read a match:

Contacts

Only 331 have a contact route

Just 5% of the base carries an email or phone number, and 209 of those are generic inboxes or bare phone numbers rather than confirmed routes. Goldo flags every one of them — check before you send anything.

Cheque sizes

Rule S4 currently never fires

No investor in the base publishes a per-deal cheque range, so the cheque-size scorer awards nothing to anyone. Goldo would rather leave a rule inert than estimate a range from fund size and present the guess as data.

Vocabulary

Translation loses detail

The source batch records stages and industries in a different vocabulary, so it is translated on import. Round types that aren't a company stage — grants, debt, secondary sales — are dropped, as are industry tags too generic to signal anything, such as “Software” and “Internet”.

Freshness

A snapshot, not a feed

The base is compiled from public sources and is not continuously refreshed. Funds change thesis, raise new vehicles and go quiet without updating anything public. Treat a match as a starting point for your own research.

None of this is hidden from the output. Goldo tells you how many investors it screened and how many the hard filters ruled out, so you can see the funnel behind your five results rather than just the five.
