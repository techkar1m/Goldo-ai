FAQ

Questions founders ask first.

Including the ones where the answer is no.

Is it free?

Yes. There's no sign-up, no account and nothing to pay. Open the chat, describe your round, get a shortlist.

Does a language model choose the investors?

No. A zero-shot classifier reads your description and fills in three slots — sector, stage and geography. That's the only place a model is involved.

Choosing and ranking investors is done by seven production rules over structured data. That separation is deliberate: it means every result can be traced to a rule and reproduced, instead of depending on how a model felt about your wording.

Will Goldo introduce me to investors?

No. Goldo doesn't contact anyone, doesn't broker introductions and has no relationship with the firms in its knowledge base. It hands you a shortlist and a contact route where one is on file; the outreach is yours to do.

Where does the investor data come from?

It was compiled by hand from public sources — firm websites, published fund information and investor directories — into 176 structured frames covering Malaysia and Singapore.

It's a snapshot, not a live feed. Funds change thesis and raise new vehicles without updating anything public, so treat a match as a research starting point. The coverage page lists exactly what's in there and where it's thin.

Why only Malaysia and Singapore?

Because the geography rules and the coverage map are built specifically for those two markets, and because the data was verified for them. A founder in Jakarta or Manila would get results computed from regional funds that happen to mention Southeast Asia — which would look plausible and be much less trustworthy. Goldo would rather cover two markets properly.

What does the score mean?

It's the sum of the weighted rules that fired, out of a maximum of 100: stage fit up to 35, geography and sector 25 each, cheque size 10, focus tag 5.

It measures how well a firm fits the facts you gave. It is not a probability that they'll invest, and there's no model of investor appetite behind it. Two firms on 70 are equally consistent with your description, nothing more. The method page shows a full worked score.

Why do I only get five results?

Because a shortlist you'll actually act on beats a ranking you'll scroll past. Goldo tells you how many investors it screened and how many the hard filters ruled out, so you can see the funnel behind the five.

If you want to widen it, change what you tell it — a broader geography or an unstated target raise will let more firms through the filters.

It misread my startup. What now?

Say it again more plainly. The classifier only accepts a slot when it clears a confidence floor, so vague descriptions tend to leave slots empty rather than fill them wrongly — and empty slots make Goldo ask you directly.

Naming things explicitly helps most: “seed stage, healthtech, Singapore, raising US$2M” will always beat a paragraph about your mission.

What if nothing matches?

Goldo says so rather than padding the list. It happens when the hard filters eliminate almost everyone — usually a stage far from where this market's capital sits, or a market outside Malaysia and Singapore. The stage breakdown shows where the pools are thin.

Can I see the whole investor list?

Not as a downloadable database — Goldo is built to answer a specific query, not to hand over a contact list for bulk outreach, which is how investor relationships get burned. The coverage page publishes the full aggregate shape of the data instead.

What happens to what I type?

Your messages are processed by Botpress, which hosts the chat, and your description is sent to a hosted classifier to fill in the three slots. The site itself has no analytics, no ad tags and no tracking cookies.

Don't paste anything confidential — the sector, stage, market and raise size are all Goldo needs. See privacy for the detail.

The chat button doesn't appear.

Almost always a privacy extension or a network blocking botpress.cloud, which is where the chat loads from. Try a private window, or allow that domain. If the widget never loads, the site tells you rather than leaving the button dead.

Is this financial advice?

No, and it can't be. Goldo is a matching tool built as a university project for module AAPP002-4-2. It doesn't know your cap table, your runway or your obligations, and nothing it outputs should be treated as investment, legal or financial advice.
