Privacy

What Goldo does with what you type.

Short version: the site itself collects nothing, the chat is hosted by Botpress, and your description passes through a hosted classifier. Don't paste anything confidential.

The site

These pages are static files served from a Node process. There is no database, no user account, no login and no contact form. The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and carries no advertising or tracking tags. Standard web-server request logs (IP address, requested path, timestamp, user agent) exist for the lifetime of the running process and are not retained or analysed.

The chat

The assistant is a Botpress bot embedded from Botpress's CDN. When you open the chat, your browser connects to botpress.cloud and everything you type in the chat window is processed on Botpress's infrastructure under their terms and privacy policy. Botpress stores the conversation so the bot can hold context across messages, and sets its own storage in your browser to keep a conversation open across page loads.

Two things follow from that. First, the operators of this project can see conversations in the Botpress dashboard. Second, deleting the conversation on your side does not necessarily remove it from Botpress.

The classifier

To turn your description into a sector, stage and geography, the bot sends the text of that description to a hosted inference API running the open facebook/bart-large-mnli model. Only the description is sent — not your name, not your email, and nothing you type later in the conversation.

What not to send

Goldo needs four facts: your sector, your stage, your market and roughly what you're raising. That's it. Please don't paste:

  • Cap tables, term sheets or signed agreements.
  • Unpublished financials or revenue figures you'd not want repeated.
  • Personal data about co-founders, employees or customers.
  • Anything covered by an NDA.

Treat the chat like a conversation at a networking event: enough to get a useful pointer, nothing you'd regret being on the record.

Investor data on our side

The knowledge base holds business contact information for investment firms, compiled from public sources — firm websites, published fund information and investor directories. It's business-to-business contact data, not personal profiles, and it isn't sold, licensed or handed out in bulk. If you represent a firm in it and want your entry corrected or removed, raise it through the project repository.

Children

This is a tool for people raising venture funding. It isn't directed at anyone under 18.

Status of this project

Goldo is a student project built for module AAPP002-4-2, not a company. There is no commercial entity behind it, no data-processing agreement on offer, and no guarantee of availability — it may be taken offline once the module ends.

Changes

If what the site does with data changes, this page changes with it. There's no mailing list to notify, so the page itself is the record.
